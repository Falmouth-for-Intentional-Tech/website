## What we're advocating for

These recommendations are adapted from [Schools Beyond Screens](https://schoolsbeyondscreens.com/reform-proposals).

- Eliminate all device use for TK–2.
- Eliminate 1:1 device use for grades 3–8, with limited device use through a computer cart or lab model where it is appropriate for the curriculum.
- Block student access to YouTube, as well as all gaming, video-streaming and social media, on student devices.
- Require all apps used for instruction or on school-issued devices to be ad-free.
- Disable student access to any generative AI tools, apps, websites and software on all school-issued devices until this technology has been proven safe, legal and effective, and until age-appropriate guidelines are established.
- Prohibit all student device use during passing periods, lunch and recess.
- Require explicit parental consent for each app that collects data, per FTC guidelines on COPPA.
- Be transparent about technology use in schools, both on devices and in teacher-led instruction.
- Create clear pathways for parents and students who wish to opt out of device-based instruction, without penalty or reprisal, and provide low-tech alternatives.
- Require all EdTech products to meet independent standards for efficacy, and to prove that any benefits outweigh potential risks.
- Prohibit screen time from being used as a reward, incentive or behavioral reinforcement for students.
