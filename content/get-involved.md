## 1. Join the group

Create a free account and join our local group. That's where we keep the member list, send updates, and coordinate. Signing up there makes sure you hear about meetings and actions.

## 2. Spread the word

- Forward this site to one family who cares about screen time and classroom tech.
- Ask your PTO/PTA to share it in their newsletter.
- Bring a friend to the next meeting.
